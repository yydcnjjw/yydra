Why Campaign C failed

        
The title was visible, but none of six submissions exposed it as a heading. The general behavior test did not ask the browser accessibility tree whether the title had that role.

      

      

        
Why no single surface is enough

        
Skill prose improves discoverability but cannot fail closed. A pattern is concrete but passive. A generic runner can enforce registered assertions, but it cannot invent missing product meaning without becoming a semantics DSL.

      

      

        
Small external interface, deep implementation

        
Product authors use direct React Native semantics and one ordinary Playwright spec. yydra check . hides runtime orchestration, zero-test/skip detection, evidence, and aggregate accounting.

      

      

        
What a pass cannot claim

        
It is H5-only evidence for visible registered assertions. It is not complete WCAG proof, native assistive-technology validation, hidden acceptance, Safe Completion, or evidence that Baseline Skills improve outcomes.
